Privacy Policy

Que Tal? - Learn Spanish (the "App") · Sham Studios · Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the App uses and why. Sham Studios, based in the United Kingdom, is the controller of your information. Contact: sfnfretail@gmail.com.

What we collect, in short

  • If you only use the App: nothing is sent to us. Progress and settings stay on your device. Ads, purchases and abuse protection (below) still involve our service providers.
  • If you sign in: account ID and name (if shared), and your monthly quiz scores.
  • From ads: approximate location, advertising and device identifiers, and ad interaction data, collected by Google AdMob.
  • From purchases: account or anonymous ID, receipts and device information, collected by RevenueCat.

Information stored only on your device

Your lessons, progress, streaks, hearts, settings and quiz high scores are stored locally on your device. Speech recognition and text-to-speech use your device's built-in services. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. The microphone is used only while you are practising speaking. We do not store your audio or receive it ourselves. However, the speech recognition built into your device (Google's on Android, Apple's on iOS) may send the audio to that provider to turn it into text, under its own privacy policy.

Account and leaderboards

You can optionally sign in with Google or Apple (via Firebase Authentication). We receive a unique account ID and, if you share them, your name and email address from Google or Apple. We do not store or show your profile photo. If you play quizzes while signed in, your display name and best score for each month are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore (hosted in London, UK) and are visible to other signed-in users on the monthly leaderboard. Monthly leaderboard entries are not used for anything else.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address) and information about how you interact with ads to show personalised ads and to measure them. This can involve tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. Premium subscribers do not see ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and some US states) you are asked for your choice, and you can change it at any time in Settings. On iOS, tracking only happens if you allow it in the system prompt, and you can change that at any time in iOS Settings. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. RevenueCat receives your account ID (or an anonymous ID), purchase receipts and device information. Subscriptions include a free trial for new subscribers and renew automatically until you cancel in your App Store or Google Play account. We never see your payment details.

Abuse protection and diagnostics

We use Firebase App Check to verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This involves device or app-integrity signals processed by Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck (iOS). We do not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service; basic diagnostic data (such as request errors and device type) is received by the Firebase, AdMob and RevenueCat services described above.

Service providers

Google (Firebase, AdMob), RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play. Each processes data under its own privacy policy and our agreements with them. We do not sell your personal information. Some of these providers are located outside the UK and EEA (for example in the United States); where that happens they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete the App. Account and leaderboard data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and leaderboard entries, use the delete option in the App's Settings (when signed in) or email sfnfretail@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

How we use your information

To run the App and your account (performance of our contract with you), to show ads and measure them (your consent, where required), to verify and protect the App against abuse (our legitimate interests), and to meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to its use, or to withdraw consent at any time. Email us to exercise these rights. If you are in the UK or EEA you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
