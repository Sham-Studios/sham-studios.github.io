Privacy Policy

Que Tal? - Learn Spanish (the "App") · Sham Studios · Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information the App uses and why. Contact: sfnfretail@gmail.com.

Information stored only on your device

Your lessons, progress, streaks, hearts, settings and quiz high scores are stored locally on your device. Speech recognition and text-to-speech use your device's built-in services. The microphone is used only while you are practising speaking. We do not record or receive your audio; however, your device's speech service (for example Google's on Android or Apple's on iOS) may process it, including on its servers, under its own privacy policy.

Account and leaderboards

You can optionally sign in with Google or Apple (via Firebase Authentication). We receive a unique account ID and, if you share them, your name and profile photo. If you play quizzes while signed in, your display name, profile photo URL and best score for each month are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and are visible to other signed-in users on the monthly leaderboard. Monthly leaderboard entries are not used for anything else.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and similar data to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and some US states) you are asked for your choice, and you can change it at any time in Settings. On iOS, tracking only happens if you allow it in the system prompt. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. RevenueCat receives your account ID (or an anonymous ID), purchase receipts and device information. We never see your payment details.

Abuse protection and diagnostics

We use Firebase App Check to verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This involves device or app-integrity signals processed by Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck (iOS).

Service providers

Google (Firebase, AdMob), RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play. Each processes data under its own privacy policy and our agreements with them. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete the App. Account and leaderboard data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and leaderboard entries, use the delete option in the App's Settings (when signed in) or email sfnfretail@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to its use. Email us to exercise these rights.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
